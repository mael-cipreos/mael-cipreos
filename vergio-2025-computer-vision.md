# Medical Imaging Models for a Mobile Health App

**Vergio Consulting, Computer Vision Intern, April to July 2025, Clichy, France**

## Context

Vergio Consulting was preparing to launch a mobile application in the medical space. The app needed image analysis that was accurate enough for a healthcare context and light enough to ship in a cross-platform mobile product. I joined for four months to build the AI side of that launch.

## What I built

I developed and trained the computer vision models behind the app's medical image processing. That meant preparing training data, choosing architectures, training and evaluating models, and iterating until the results were stable enough for a medical use case.

On the deployment side, I worked across Android Studio and Xcode to keep the cross-platform build workflow moving, and I evaluated model behavior directly in our development environment so problems were caught before they reached a device.

## Stack

Python, TensorFlow, Keras, OpenCV, Android Studio, Xcode, Visual Studio Code

## Results

- Delivered the trained image processing models the launch depended on, which helped accelerate the app's time to market.
- Set up a repeatable workflow for training, evaluating, and deploying models across both mobile platforms.

## What I took away

Medical products have a low tolerance for "mostly right." This internship taught me to treat evaluation as seriously as training, and to think about deployment constraints from the first experiment instead of at the end.
